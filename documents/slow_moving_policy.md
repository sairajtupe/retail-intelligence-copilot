# Slow-Moving and Dead Stock Policy

## SLM-01: Slow-Moving Threshold
A product is classified as slow-moving when units sold in the trailing 30 days is less than 10% of current stock, and days of cover exceeds 90.

## SLM-02: Dead Stock Threshold
A product is classified as dead stock when no units have been sold in the trailing 30 days and current stock exceeds the minimum order quantity.

## SLM-03: Root Cause Investigation
Before actioning slow-moving stock, investigate whether the issue is demand-related (actual low customer interest), supply-related (stock arrived in wrong quantities), or data-related (stock records inaccurate).

## SLM-04: Action Escalation
Slow-moving items: apply markdown policy after 60 days of excess cover. Dead stock items: begin markdown or liquidation process within 14 days of classification.

## SLM-05: Value Impact Reporting
For every slow-moving or dead stock classification, calculate and report: (1) units in inventory, (2) inventory value at cost, (3) estimated days to clear at current demand, (4) recommended action.
