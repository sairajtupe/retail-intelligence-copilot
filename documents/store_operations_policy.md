# Store Operations Policy

## SOP-01: Store Performance Monitoring
Each store should be reviewed weekly for revenue performance, inventory health, and operational metrics. Stores with revenue declining more than 10% month-over-month require investigation.

## SOP-02: Cross-Store Comparisons
When comparing stores, normalise for store size by using per-unit metrics (revenue per unit sold, margin per unit) rather than absolute totals.

## SOP-03: Store Category Mix
Each store's category performance should be benchmarked against the company average. Stores where a category underperforms the average by more than 25% may have local demand issues or merchandising problems.

## SOP-04: Regional Demand Patterns
Regional factors (climate, demographics, events) may legitimately create demand differences between stores. These should be documented and not treated as anomalies.

## SOP-05: Inventory Balance
A healthy inventory balance across the network is one where no store holds more than 40% of the total network stock for any given product, unless justified by local demand share.

## TRF-01: Inter-Store Transfer Criteria
An inter-store transfer is recommended when: (1) the source store has cover days exceeding the destination's by more than 100%, AND (2) the destination store has cover days below the supplier lead time, AND (3) both stores carry the same product.

## TRF-02: Transfer Quantity Limits
Transfer quantity should not exceed the excess stock at the source (stock minus 30 days of demand at source) or the deficit at the destination (target stock minus current stock), whichever is lower.

## TRF-03: Proximity Factor
Transfers between stores in the same region are preferred. Cross-region transfers require a cost-benefit analysis showing the transfer cost is below the value of lost sales avoided.

## TRF-04: Transfer Feasibility
Only products with shelf life exceeding 60 days and no fragile handling requirements are eligible for transfer. Perishable items may only be transferred if they retain at least 70% of remaining shelf life at the destination.

## TRF-05: Data Sufficiency
A transfer recommendation requires at least 14 days of demand data at both the source and destination stores. With insufficient history, suppress the transfer recommendation.
